    {JVT: Just Video Transformer Makes Pixel-Space Video Diffusion Simple}
    {Jianjun Zhou\eq, \me\eq, Haoyu Guo\corr}
    {Under Review}
    {Introduces a simple DiT-style Transformer for pixel-space video diffusion, without a pretrained autoencoder, cascaded stages or U-Net skip connections, achieving quality competitive with latent diffusion baselines. Its pixel-space formulation also enables joint camera-conditioned RGB-disparity generation, improving camera following and yielding explicit scene geometry.}

  \publication[https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21713]
    {Unlocking the Power of Critical Factors for 3D Visual Geometry Estimation}
    {Guangkai Xu\eq, \me\eq, Huanyi Zheng, Songyi Yin, Yanlong Sun, Hao Chen, Chunhua Shen\corr}
    {CVPR 2026}
    {Systematically studies the critical factors in feed-forward visual geometry estimation (data scaling, loss design, alignment supervision) and introduces CARVE, a resolution-enhanced model with a depth-camera-point-map consistency loss, achieving strong results on point cloud reconstruction, video depth and camera pose estimation.}
